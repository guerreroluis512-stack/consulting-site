About Luis

Turning policy into practice, and practice into results

I've worked at every level of New York City Public Schools: as a teacher, a World Language Department Chair, a Dean of Students, and now as a designer of system-wide professional learning alongside superintendents.

My approach is data-informed, collaborative, and systems-oriented. I help leaders turn big priorities into coherent, lasting practice in classrooms, schools, and communities.

FAQ

Common questions

Who do you work with?

School districts and superintendent teams, principals and school leadership teams, community-based and nonprofit organizations, teacher preparation programs, and family and youth programs.

Can you facilitate in Spanish?

Yes. I'm fully bilingual and can design and deliver sessions, workshops, and family programs in English, Spanish, or both.

What does a typical engagement look like?

It can be a single workshop, a multi-session professional learning series, or an ongoing partnership. We'll start with a conversation about your goals, and I'll recommend the format that fits your needs.

How do we get started?

Send me a message using the form below. Tell me a bit about your organization and what you're hoping to accomplish, and I'll follow up to set up an introductory conversation.
