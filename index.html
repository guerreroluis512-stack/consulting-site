About

Turning policy into practice, and practice into results

I'm an equity-driven educational leader. My work in New York City Public Schools has spanned the classroom, school leadership, and the central office. I've been a teacher, a World Language Department Chair, and a Dean of Students. Today I design system-wide professional learning in partnership with superintendents.

My approach is data-informed, collaborative, and systems-oriented. I help leaders turn district and organizational priorities into coherent, lasting practice in classrooms, schools, and communities.

  • Systems thinking
  • Adult learning design
  • Restorative practices
  • Multilingual learner advocacy
  • Community schools
  • Cross-sector partnerships
  • Data-informed improvement

Experience

Leadership across the system

2026 – Present

Program Associate of Professional Learning

NYC Public Schools, Office of Student Pathways

I co-design system-wide professional learning with superintendents, aligned to the NYCPS 2030 Pathways vision. I lead cross-district strategy through the annual Summer Institute and help district teams scale initiatives in financial literacy, early college, computer science, advising, and career readiness across 45 superintendent portfolios.

2025 – Present

Lead Instructor

New York City Teaching Fellows

I design and facilitate daily training for aspiring teachers and give them targeted feedback on planning, delivery, and engagement. I also help with recruitment and support systems. 100% of the teachers in my cohort completed the program.

2023 – Present

Professional Development Facilitator

GenConnect / Puerto Rican Hispanic Youth Leadership Institute

I run an 8-week training for newly arrived families, build professional learning for principals serving newcomer families, and prepare Hispanic youth for a leadership retreat in Albany through a train-the-trainer model.

2025 – 2026

Dean of Students / Coordinator of Culture & Climate

Gotham Tech High School, Queens

I built systems for attendance, behavior, and engagement, put restorative justice practices in place, and led initiatives that strengthened community and shared values among students and staff.

2021 – 2025

World Language Department Chair
