My story

From newcomer to leader

In 2012, I arrived in the United States as an asylum seeker from El Salvador. On Long Island, my ENL teacher guided me through a new language and a new life. Her belief in me planted the seed for my career, and showed me that school can be a safe harbor, a launchpad, and a source of empowerment.

Since then, I've worked at every level of New York City Public Schools: as a Spanish teacher who designed three curricula (Spanish 1, Native Spanish, and AP Spanish), a Department Chair, a Coordinator of Student Life, a Dean of Students, and now a designer of system-wide professional learning alongside superintendents.

I see my own story in the students and families I serve. It shapes my belief that education must be rigorous, inclusive, and deeply human. My approach is data-informed, collaborative, and systems-oriented, and I help leaders turn big priorities into coherent, lasting practice.
